# Editorial typography

Use only the directions from the supplied Material 3 sources. Do not invent missing values or behavior.

Read the [source manifest](../m3-styles-sources.json) for source coverage.
Open the supplied source pages for tables, diagrams, and token modules.

Source: [Typography: Editorial treatments](https://m3.material.io/styles/typography/editorial-treatments).

## Choose editorial moments

- Use editorial treatments to celebrate content, express the user, or highlight bespoke functionality.
- Reserve these treatments for standalone showcase moments rather than basic functional layouts.
- Do not use editorial treatments for labels or merely to provide information.
- Use personalization selectively to frame the user's mood.
- Match the text's emotional tone to the task.
- Keep similar editorial moments consistent and create tokens where needed.
- Avoid multiple clashing treatments within one layout.
- Do not mimic personalization theming.

## Adjust variable axes

- Use weight to change stroke thickness.
- Avoid very light small body text, especially on lower-resolution displays.
- Avoid excessive weight at small sizes.
- Use grade for fine thickness changes without changing letter widths or line breaks.
- Consider negative grade when light text on dark backgrounds appears heavier.
- Use width to fit more characters or express personality.
- Avoid wide styles in constrained areas such as top app bars.
- Match optical size to the actual type size.
- Do not render small text with an optical design intended for large type.
- Baseline and emphasized styles can still support editorial treatments.

## Verify

Check treatment consistency, task fit, legibility, available width, line breaks, and light/dark appearance.

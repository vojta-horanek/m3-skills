# Material 3 foundation principles

Use these principles in design, development, collaboration, and evaluation.

## Accessibility by default

Source: [Principles](https://m3.material.io/foundations/overview/principles).

- Anticipate low vision, blindness, hearing, cognitive, and motor impairments, including situational disabilities.
- Account for varied human experiences before defining solutions.
- Support individual adaptation through preferences and choices. A single default experience rarely meets every need.
- Account for needs that change during a day, year, or lifetime.
- Learn from people with varied abilities and experiences before defining solutions. Use formal and informal research.
- When unexpected problems occur, use those outcomes to learn and adapt.
- Treat minimum WCAG requirements as a starting point for meeting human needs.

## Foundation fields

Source: [Foundations](https://m3.material.io/foundations).

Material foundations cover accessibility, writing, information design, interaction states, layout, and inclusive participation.
Tokens carry shared style values across design, code, tools, and platforms.
States communicate the status of interactive elements. Layout arranges elements on a screen.

For a field-specific task, use the relevant skill listed in the [source manifest](../m3-foundation-sources.json).

## Review

- Check whether research covers different abilities and life experiences.
- Check whether individuals can adapt the experience to their changing needs.
- Check whether accessibility influenced decisions before solutions were defined.

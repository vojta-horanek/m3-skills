# Grids and spacing

## Foundation

Source: [Grids and spacing overview](https://m3.material.io/foundations/layout/grids-spacing/overview).

Use grids for a consistent adaptive structure.
Use spacing to group information and direct attention to key actions.
Information density can help people compare data-heavy content.

## Grid and rulers

Source: [Grids](https://m3.material.io/foundations/layout/grids-spacing/grids).

- Start by placing grid columns. Adapt column count, width, and spacing across breakpoints.
- Place edge regions such as bars, navigation, toolbars, and app bars before filling the main region with panes.
- Use canonical layouts to choose appropriate panes.
- Use global rulers to align layout layers and establish consistent focal points.
- Use bar and safety rulers to protect actionable content from system UI.
- Use title rulers to align app bar titles, icons, and related elements.
- Use the first content ruler for major blocks, such as hero images, headlines, and primary components.
- Use secondary content rulers for supporting text and actions.
- Adjust margin rulers to control negative space and content emphasis.
- Media and controls can use different margins, such as a full-width photo grid beside an inset search control.

## Grouping and attention

Source: [Spacing](https://m3.material.io/foundations/layout/grids-spacing/spacing).

- Tie related content together through spacing. Separate unrelated content.
- Use outlines, dividers, or shadows for explicit grouping.
- Use proximity and open space for implicit grouping.
- Keep spacing consistent between related elements and groups to establish rhythm.
- Give similar elements consistent size and spacing.
- Align leading thumbnails, avatars, and icons.
- Put actions near the content they affect.
- Use rows, columns, or containers to establish continuity among related items.
- Give important content and actions generous space and prominent surfaces.
- Keep key actions and information in consistent locations across screens.
- Use negative space to frame and emphasize important elements.

## Verify grouping

Check global alignment, consistent spacing, related groups, action proximity, and recognizable focal points.
